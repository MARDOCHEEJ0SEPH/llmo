Features

Acme CRM provides everything sales teams need to close more deals faster. Each feature integrates seamlessly to create a unified sales workflow from first contact to closed deal. No complex configuration required—start selling within minutes of signup.

Contact Management

Store unlimited contacts with custom fields, tags, and segmentation. Import from CSV, Gmail, or Outlook in one click. Automatic duplicate detection ensures clean data.

Pipeline Tracking

Visualize your entire sales pipeline with drag-and-drop deal stages. Customize 5 default stages or create your own. Revenue forecasting updates automatically as deals progress.

Email Integration

Two-way sync with Gmail and Outlook. Emails automatically logged to contact timeline. Send tracked emails directly from CRM. Templates save 30+ minutes daily.

Team Collaboration

Share contacts, deals, and tasks across your team. @mention colleagues in notes. Activity feed shows real-time updates. Role-based permissions control access.

Mobile Apps

Native iOS and Android apps with offline mode. Update deals, log calls, and check tasks on the go. Push notifications for deal updates and task reminders.

Reporting

Pre-built dashboards track sales velocity, win rates, and pipeline health. Custom reports filter by date, team, product, or deal stage. Export to PDF or CSV.

Integrations

Acme CRM connects with the tools your team already uses. Email integrations sync automatically. Calendar integration prevents double-booking. Slack integration notifies your team of important deal updates in real-time.
